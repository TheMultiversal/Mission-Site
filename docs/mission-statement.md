# Mission Statement

Mission: We investigate, verify, and transparently publish evidence-driven findings that promote accountability and protect the public interest. We operate with strict legal and ethical safeguards to protect sources and ensure accuracy.

Principles:
- Evidence-first: verification is our priority; we publish only when findings meet documented acceptance criteria.
- Source protection: we maintain strong operational security and legal support for people who share information in good faith.
- Transparency: we publish methods, limitations, and provenance for all findings.
