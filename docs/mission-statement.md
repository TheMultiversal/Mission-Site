# Mission — Accountability Project

We identify, verify, and publish evidence of activities that pose public health, environmental, or governance risks. Using reproducible technical analysis, independent laboratory verification, and legal review, we produce defensible reports and actionable recommendations that enable accountability, remediation, and informed policy decisions.

## How we work

- Collect and triage community submissions and tips.
- Perform technical verification and independent laboratory testing.
- Obtain legal review and counsel to guide responsible disclosure and source protection.
- Publish reproducible reports and archived artifacts for independent scrutiny and use in policy or legal processes.
- Engage partners to pursue remediation, policy reform, and support for affected communities.
